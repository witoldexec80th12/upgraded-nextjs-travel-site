The user proposed dividing daily scans by booking system: Dibona, Foundation,
Bukly and Südtirol, with more systems added as needed. They proposed starting
the cron jobs half an hour apart in the early morning, “CET.”

**Why:** The user described this as their preferred likely approach.

**How to apply:** Use provider grouping when discussing the daily scheduler, but
do not treat the four current families as a permanent limit. Exact times and
whether “CET” means fixed UTC+1 or Italian local time with daylight saving have
not been confirmed. The audit was approved before implementing the schedule.
